Mortgage Rates by Year: 30-Year Fixed Averages Since 1971

By Mike Van Kempen Updated November 2026 7 min read

Yearly averages from Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey via FRED (30-year) and FRED (15-year); latest weekly rates from Freddie Mac. Checked November 27, 2026.

The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate in 2026 is 6.57% so far, based on Freddie Mac weekly data through November 25, and the latest weekly rate is 7.21%. Since 1971, yearly averages have ranged from a record 16.64% in 1981 to a record low of 2.96% in 2021. The long-run average of every weekly reading since 1971 is 7.68%.

Key Takeaways

  • The 30-year fixed mortgage rate has averaged 6.57% in 2026 through November 25, after 6.60% in 2025 and 6.72% in 2024, according to Freddie Mac data published by FRED.
  • The highest yearly average for a 30-year mortgage was 16.64% in 1981, and the record weekly high was 18.63% in the week of October 9, 1981.
  • The lowest yearly average was 2.96% in 2021, and the record weekly low was 2.65% in the week of January 7, 2021.
  • On November 25, 2026, the 30-year fixed rate averaged 7.21% and the 15-year fixed 6.54%, up from 6.23% and 5.51% a year earlier.
  • At 7.21%, the monthly principal and interest on a $300,000, 30-year loan is about $2,038, compared with $1,258 at 2021's average rate of 2.96%.

Freddie Mac says the survey reflects conventional, conforming home purchase loans for borrowers with good or excellent credit who put 20% down, so the rate you are offered can be higher or lower. Since November 17, 2022, the survey has used applications submitted to Freddie Mac's Loan Product Advisor system instead of a survey of lenders, and it no longer reports average fees and points. To compare total borrowing cost, look at the APR (annual percentage rate) on a lender's Loan Estimate; our APR vs. APY guide explains the difference.

Across all 2,905 weekly readings from April 1971 to November 25, 2026, the 30-year rate averaged 7.68%. By that long-run yardstick, today's rate of 7.21% is slightly below average, even though it is far above the rates of 2012 to 2021.

Record Highs and Lows

  • Record high: 18.63%. The 30-year rate peaked in the week of October 9, 1981. The 1981 yearly average of 16.64% is also the highest on record.
  • Record low: 2.65%. The 30-year rate bottomed out in the week of January 7, 2021. The 2021 yearly average of 2.96% is the lowest on record.
  • 15-year extremes. Since the 15-year series began in August 1991, its weekly high was 8.89% (December 16, 1994) and its low was 2.10% (July 29, 2021).
  • The fastest jump. The yearly average rose from 2.96% in 2021 to 6.81% in 2023, an increase of 3.85 percentage points in two years.

Where Mortgage Rates Are Now (November 2026)

In Freddie Mac's survey released November 25, 2026, the 30-year fixed rate averaged 7.21% and the 15-year fixed averaged 6.54%. A year earlier, in the November 26, 2025 survey, they were 6.23% and 5.51%. That is an increase of about 1 percentage point on both loans in 12 months.

How to Use Rate History When You Buy or Refinance

  1. Compare lenders, not just averages. The PMMS figure is a national average. Get Loan Estimates from several lenders on the same day and compare both the rate and the APR.
  2. Weigh a 15-year loan. The 15-year rate has been below the 30-year rate in every year since 1992, but the monthly payment is higher because you repay in half the time.
  3. Plan for today's rate. Nobody knows where rates will go next. Make sure the payment works at the rate you are offered now, and treat a future refinance as a bonus, not a plan.
  4. Build your down payment and credit. A larger down payment and a higher credit score usually earn a lower rate. Our how to save for a down payment guide covers the steps, and rent vs. buy compares the monthly costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average 30-year mortgage rate in 2026?

The 30-year fixed rate has averaged 6.57% in 2026, based on Freddie Mac's 47 weekly survey readings through November 25, 2026. The latest weekly rate, released November 25, was 7.21%.

What was the highest mortgage rate in history?

The highest weekly 30-year fixed rate in Freddie Mac's survey was 18.63%, in the week of October 9, 1981. The highest yearly average was 16.64%, also in 1981.

What was the lowest mortgage rate ever?

The lowest weekly 30-year fixed rate was 2.65%, in the week of January 7, 2021. The 2021 yearly average of 2.96% is the lowest on record since the survey began in 1971.

What is the average mortgage rate over the long run?

Averaging every weekly 30-year reading from April 1971 through November 25, 2026 gives 7.68%. By decade, the average was 12.71% in the 1980s, 6.29% in the 2000s and 4.09% in the 2010s.

Are 15-year mortgage rates lower than 30-year rates?

Yes. The 15-year fixed rate has had a lower yearly average than the 30-year rate in every year since 1992. On November 25, 2026, it averaged 6.54% compared with 7.21% for the 30-year loan.
