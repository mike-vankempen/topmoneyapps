Salary to Live Comfortably by State (2026)

📅 Updated August 2026 ⏱ 7 min read ✍️ By Mike Van Kempen 📊 Source: SmartAsset / MIT

"Comfortable" now costs a lot more than most people think. In 2026, a single adult needs between $81,000 and $129,000 a year to live comfortably — and in 23 states, a $100,000 salary isn't enough for one person. Hawaii tops the list at $129,002; West Virginia is cheapest at $81,245. Here's the full 50-state ranking for a single adult, plus what a family of four needs. 📌 Key Insight

"Comfortable" here isn't luxury — it's the 50/30/20 budget, where you cover your needs, have some room for wants, and still save 20%. The eye-opener is that in nearly half the country, a single person needs a six-figure salary just to hit that baseline. It's a stark reminder that a "good salary" is relative: $90,000 goes a long way in West Virginia and barely covers one person comfortably in Hawaii or California.

Make Your Salary Go Further

These are benchmarks, not destiny — your savings rate and spending choices matter as much as the number on your paycheck. Two levers move the needle most: earning more (see average salary by state) and lowering your cost base (see cost of living by state). And wherever you land on the income scale, a budget is what turns "comfortable on paper" into comfortable in reality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a single person need to live comfortably in 2026? ▼

Between about $81,000 and $129,000 a year depending on the state, under the 50/30/20 rule. In 23 states, $100,000 isn't enough for one person. Hawaii is highest ($129,002); West Virginia lowest ($81,245).

Which state requires the highest income? ▼

Hawaii at $129,002 for a single adult, then Massachusetts ($127,213), California ($126,797), and New York ($124,342). For a family of four, Massachusetts is highest at nearly $330,000.

Which state is cheapest? ▼

West Virginia at $81,245 for a single adult, followed by Arkansas ($83,242), Kentucky ($84,074), and Louisiana ($84,739) — mostly Southern and Midwestern states.

How much does a family of four need? ▼

At least $200,000 a year in most states. Massachusetts is highest at ~$330,000; Mississippi lowest at ~$187,533. Childcare drives most of the increase over a single adult.

What does "live comfortably" mean here? ▼

The 50/30/20 budget: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings/debt. So it covers necessities plus saving and some discretionary spending — not just scraping by. Figures are pre-tax, from MIT living-wage data (Feb 2026).
