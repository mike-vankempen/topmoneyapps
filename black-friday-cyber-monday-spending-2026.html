Black Friday & Cyber Monday 2026: Spending Forecast & Dates

📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 5 min read 📊 Sources: Adobe, NRF

📋 Sources: Adobe Analytics 2026 Holiday Forecast · National Retail Federation

In 2026, Black Friday is November 27 and Cyber Monday is November 30. Adobe forecasts $12.9 billion in online spending on Black Friday (up 9.2%) and $15.1 billion on Cyber Monday — the single biggest online day of the year. The full five-day Cyber Week is projected at $47.5 billion. Here are the numbers, the best days to buy, and how to shop the sales without a January hangover. Black Friday vs. Cyber Monday: Which Is Better?

Cyber Monday is still the biggest online day overall, but the "best" day depends on what you're buying:

CategoryBetter DayWhy
TVs & appliancesBlack FridayRetailers lead with big-ticket in-store doorbusters.
Electronics & techCyber MondayOnline-only flash deals peak on Monday.
Toys & apparelEitherPrices are similar across the weekend.
TravelCyber Monday"Travel Deal Tuesday" and Cyber Monday fares dip.

Bottom line: for most products the prices are nearly identical Friday through Monday. Buy when you see your target price rather than gambling on a specific day — and don't talk yourself into something just because it's "on sale."

The Catch: Buy Now, Pay Later

Adobe expects $21.3 billion in buy-now-pay-later spending this season, with more than $1 billion on Cyber Monday alone. BNPL feels free in the moment, but splitting a purchase into four payments can quietly turn a one-day deal into months of debt — especially if you do it across several purchases. Use it only when you can clear the balance in the zero-interest window.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Black Friday and Cyber Monday 2026?

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26, Black Friday is Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30. "Cyber Week" is the five-day run from Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday.

How much will Americans spend on Black Friday 2026?

Adobe forecasts about $12.9 billion online on Black Friday (+9.2%), with Cyber Monday larger at $15.1 billion and the full Cyber Week at $47.5 billion — roughly 17% of the season's online spending.

Is Black Friday or Cyber Monday better for deals?

Cyber Monday is the biggest online day and best for electronics and tech; Black Friday wins for TVs, appliances, and in-store big-ticket items. For most products prices are similar all weekend, so buy at your target price rather than waiting for one day.

What is Cyber Week spending in 2026?

Adobe projects $47.5 billion online during Cyber Week (+7.4%): Thanksgiving ~$6.9B, Black Friday ~$12.9B, Cyber Monday ~$15.1B.

How can I avoid overspending on Black Friday?

List your target prices before the sales, confirm "deals" with a price tracker, set a hard budget, pay with a cash-back card you'll clear in full, and be cautious with BNPL — Adobe expects over $1B in BNPL on Cyber Monday alone.
