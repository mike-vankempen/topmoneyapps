Average Cell Phone Bill (2026): Monthly Cost by State

By Mike Van Kempen Updated November 2026 7 min read

State figures from the doxo 2026 state-by-state bill pay report; household spending from the BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey; price trends from the BLS CPI. Checked November 2026.

The typical U.S. household pays a median $100 a month ($1,200 a year) for cell phone service, based on doxo's 2026 bill pay data. Government survey data put the average a little higher: households spent $1,390 on cellular phone service in 2025, about $116 a month, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Bills range from a median $77 a month in Wisconsin to $133 in Montana, and wireless prices rose 3.2% in the year to September 2026.

Average vs. Median Cell Phone Bill

Two good sources measure what households pay, and they count it differently. doxo, a bill pay company, reports the median (middle) bill among households that pay one. The BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey reports the mean (average) across all households, including households that pay no mobile bill of their own. doxo finds 12% of households have no mobile bill.

What Carriers Report per Account and per Line

The big three carriers report how much service revenue they collect per account (ARPA) or per phone line (ARPU) in their quarterly earnings. These are carrier averages, not bills. They leave out taxes, fees collected for the government and device installment payments, so your actual bill is often higher.

Postpaid means you pay after the month of service, usually on a monthly plan billed to an account. Prepaid means you pay up front each month. Verizon's prepaid revenue per line is well under its postpaid revenue per account, but a postpaid account often includes several lines, so the two are not a direct comparison.

How Fast Cell Phone Bills Are Rising

  • 3.2% in a year. The BLS consumer price index for wireless telephone services rose 3.2% from September 2025 to September 2026, according to the September 2026 CPI release. The index tracks the price of service plans only. It leaves out phones and device fees and adjusts for changes in plan features such as data.
  • 2.3% in household spending. Average household spending on cellular phone service rose from $1,359 in 2024 to $1,390 in 2025 in the BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey. It was $1,244 in 2021, $1,284 in 2022 and $1,270 in 2023.
  • $96 to $100. doxo's median mobile bill rose from $96 a month in its July 2025 report to $100 in its 2026 reports.

How to Lower Your Cell Phone Bill

  1. Check how much data you use. Your carrier's app or account page shows your monthly data use. If you use far less than your plan allows, a smaller plan may cost less.
  2. Compare prepaid plans. Prepaid plans, including those from smaller carriers that run on the big networks (called MVNOs, or mobile virtual network operators), can cost less than postpaid plans. Verizon's core prepaid customers brought in $33.51 a month per line in Q3 2026. Check coverage in your area and any limits on data speed before you switch.
  3. Separate the phone from the plan. A device installment can add a lot to a monthly bill. Keeping your current phone longer, or buying one outright, keeps the service cost visible.
  4. Apply for Lifeline if you qualify. The FCC's Lifeline program gives eligible households up to $9.25 a month off phone or internet service, or up to $34.25 on Tribal lands. You can qualify with income at or below 135% of the federal poverty guidelines, or by taking part in SNAP, Medicaid, Federal Public Housing Assistance, SSI or the Veterans and Survivors Pension Benefit. Only one Lifeline benefit is allowed per household, and you apply through the National Verifier at nv.fcc.gov.
  5. Review the bill line by line. Look for add-ons you no longer need, such as device insurance or extra lines, and cancel what you do not use.

Your phone bill is one line in your monthly budget. Compare it with our average monthly expenses, average subscription spending and average electric bill by state pages. A budgeting app can help you track recurring bills month to month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cell phone bill per month?

The median U.S. household mobile phone bill is $100 a month, or $1,200 a year, according to doxo's 2026 bill pay data. The BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey shows households spent an average of $1,390 on cellular phone service in 2025, about $116 a month.

Which state has the highest cell phone bill?

Montana, with a median mobile bill of $133 a month in doxo's May 2026 state-by-state report, followed by Alaska and Kentucky at $125. doxo's September 2026 mobile report ranks Delaware highest at a median $1,630 a year.

Which state has the cheapest cell phone bill?

Wisconsin, with a median mobile bill of $77 a month in doxo's May 2026 state-by-state report, followed by Tennessee at $82 and Illinois and Kansas at $83.

Are cell phone bills going up?

Yes, modestly. The BLS price index for wireless telephone services rose 3.2% from September 2025 to September 2026, and average household spending on cellular service rose from $1,359 in 2024 to $1,390 in 2025.

Can I get a discount on my phone bill if I have a low income?

Yes. The FCC's Lifeline program gives eligible households up to $9.25 a month off phone or internet service, or up to $34.25 on Tribal lands. You can qualify with income at or below 135% of the federal poverty guidelines or by taking part in programs such as SNAP or Medicaid.
