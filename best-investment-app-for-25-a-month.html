Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best investment app for $25 a month? ▼

Acorns, for most beginners — it automatically invests your $25 plus round-ups into a diversified ETF portfolio with no stock-picking. Fidelity and SoFi are strong $0-fee alternatives if you prefer a more hands-on setup.

Is $25 a month worth investing? ▼

Yes. At 7% a year, $25/month grows to ~$4,300 in 10 years, ~$13,000 in 20, and ~$30,000 in 30 — from just $9,000 contributed. The habit matters more than the amount.

Does Acorns charge a fee on $25 a month? ▼

Acorns starts at a flat $4/month. On a tiny balance that's a high percentage, so a $0-fee broker like Fidelity may cost less if you only ever invest $25. Acorns wins when you value the automation and plan to grow contributions over time.

Can you actually invest just $25 a month? ▼

Yes — fractional shares let $25 buy a slice of a diversified portfolio. Acorns, Fidelity, SoFi, and Robinhood all support recurring $25 investments.

How do I start investing $25 a month? ▼

Open an account, link your bank, set a $25 automatic recurring investment on payday, pick a diversified portfolio (Acorns does this for you), and let it compound.
