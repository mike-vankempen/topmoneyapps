Is Databricks publicly traded?

No. Databricks — the data lakehouse and AI platform used by thousands of enterprises — remains privately held. It has not filed a public S-1 with the SEC, has not announced an exchange or ticker, and its CEO has repeatedly said it will stay private as long as it can raise capital on good terms. That means there is no legitimate way to buy Databricks shares through a standard brokerage account like Robinhood, Fidelity, or Schwab right now.

⚠️ Scam warning: Hot private AI names attract fraud. Scammers advertise "pre-IPO Databricks shares" or "guaranteed allocations." There is no public Databricks stock and no official retail IPO allocation. Never send money to anyone promising Databricks IPO shares — verify everything through the SEC's EDGAR database and your own broker.

How to be ready for the Databricks IPO

  1. Open a brokerage account that offers IPO access. Brokers like Robinhood, SoFi, and Webull sometimes give everyday investors a shot at IPO shares at the offering price. Set this up before any listing.
  2. Keep some cash settled and ready. IPO windows move fast. Settled cash lets you act when shares open.
  3. Watch for the official S-1. When Databricks files, the prospectus reveals real financials, the ticker, and the exchange. That's your signal — not a social-media rumor.
  4. Decide your rules in advance. First-day IPO prices are volatile. Know your max price and position size before you click buy, and remember many brokers restrict flipping IPO shares for ~30 days.
