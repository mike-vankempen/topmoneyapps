Acorns vs. Alinea (2026): Which Beginner Investing App Is Worth Paying For?

📅 Updated November 22, 2026 ⏱ 8 min read ✍️ By Mike Van Kempen

Acorns and Alinea both charge a subscription instead of commissions, and both are built for people making their first investment. The difference is what the subscription buys. Acorns ($4–$12/month) bundles automated ETF investing, an IRA, checking and kids' accounts. Alinea ($120/year) is an iPhone investing app built around themed "playlists," crypto and an AI assistant. Here's how they compare on cost, features and safety.

Key Takeaways

  • Acorns costs $4, $8 or $12 a month ($48–$144 a year). Alinea costs $120 a year after a 7-day free trial, with no per-trade commissions.
  • Acorns is the better pick for hands-off beginners: automatic Round-Ups into diversified ETF portfolios, plus an IRA, checking and (on Gold) kids' accounts in one app.
  • Alinea is the better pick for iPhone users who want to choose themes, individual stocks and crypto, with an AI assistant (Allie) explaining their portfolio.
  • On small balances both fees are steep: $120 a year is 12% of a $1,000 account, versus 4.8% for Acorns Bronze.
  • Both hold brokerage accounts with SIPC protection up to $500,000. Alinea is iPhone-only; Acorns runs on iPhone, Android and the web.

Acorns vs. Alinea: Quick Verdict

Choose Acorns if you want investing to happen in the background. Acorns rounds up your card purchases and invests the spare change, along with any recurring deposits, into a diversified ETF portfolio you never have to manage. The same subscription covers a retirement account (Acorns Later) and a checking account, and Bronze is cheaper than Alinea.

Choose Alinea if you want to be involved in what you own. Alinea lets you invest in curated themes, pick individual stocks and ETFs, buy crypto, or use a portfolio its investment team builds around your goals. It's designed for people who want investing to feel personal and social, and who use an iPhone.

⭐ Our Verdict

For most first-time investors, Acorns is the stronger value: it costs less at the entry tier, covers retirement and banking, and automates the habit that matters most, which is investing consistently. Alinea is worth its $120 only if you'll actually use the themes, crypto and AI tools, and your balance is large enough that the fee is a small share of it.

Fees: What You Actually Pay

Neither app charges per trade, so the subscription is the whole cost. Acorns' three plans work out to $48 a year (Bronze), $96 a year (Silver) and $144 a year (Gold). Alinea has one plan at $120 a year, billed after a 7-day free trial. The College Investor notes that Alinea doesn't offer refunds, so cancel during the trial if you're not sure.

Acorns: Best for Automatic, All-in-One Investing

Acorns turns investing into something that happens without a decision. Link a card, and every purchase is rounded up to the next dollar; the spare change, plus any recurring deposits, goes into a diversified portfolio of ETFs from providers including Vanguard and BlackRock's iShares, rebalanced automatically. You can start Acorns Invest with $5.

What Acorns does well

  • Round-Ups and recurring investments: the core habit-builder, running in the background.
  • Retirement included: Acorns Later offers Traditional, Roth and SEP IRAs on every plan, with a 1% (Silver) or 3% (Gold) match on new contributions during your first subscription year.
  • Banking: Acorns Checking with no overdraft fees and fee-free withdrawals at 55,000+ Allpoint ATMs; Silver adds an Emergency Savings account (3.59% APY as of September 22, 2026; variable).
  • Family features (Gold): custodial investment accounts for kids, the Acorns Early kids' debit card, and Custom Portfolios for adding individual stocks and ETFs.
  • Scale: Acorns reports more than 16 million all-time customers and over $33 billion invested (as of August 26, 2026).

Acorns drawbacks

  • The flat fee is a large percentage of small balances (see the table above).
  • Picking individual stocks requires the $12/month Gold plan.
  • No direct crypto; bitcoin exposure comes only through a bitcoin-linked ETF in the portfolio.

Alinea: Best for Theme-Based Investing on iPhone

Alinea was founded in 2020 by Columbia students Eve Halimi and Anam Lakhani and is aimed squarely at Gen Z and first-time investors. Instead of a single automated portfolio, you build your own from curated "playlists" (themes such as AI, climate or women-led companies), individual stocks, ETFs and crypto, or let Alinea's investment team build a portfolio around the goals and risk tolerance you state during onboarding.

What Alinea does well

  • Themed playlists: invest in ideas you understand, and build, share or copy playlists with other users.
  • Crypto alongside stocks: buy cryptocurrency in the same app as your stocks and ETFs, with fractional shares and no account minimum.
  • Allie, the AI assistant: explains your portfolio's performance and composition, plus a daily briefing and goal projections. Alinea describes this as educational information, not personalized advice.
  • Highly rated app: 4.7 out of 5 from roughly 124,000 ratings on the App Store (November 2026).

Alinea drawbacks

  • $120 a year is more than Acorns Bronze or Silver, and much more than free brokers like Robinhood or Fidelity.
  • iPhone only: no Android app and no web or desktop access.
  • No banking, kids' accounts or IRA match. Alinea's App Store listing announces Roth and Traditional IRAs, but several 2026 reviews still describe it as taxable-only, so confirm in the app before you sign up for retirement investing.
  • Support is email and in-app chat only, with no phone line.
  • Theme investing and copying other users' playlists can concentrate your money in a few trends. Treat themes as a slice of a diversified portfolio, not the whole thing.

Safety: Are Acorns and Alinea Legit?

Yes, both are regulated. Acorns Advisers is an SEC-registered investment adviser and Acorns Securities is an SEC-registered broker-dealer and SIPC member, so brokerage securities are protected up to $500,000; Acorns Checking deposits are FDIC-insured up to $250,000 through its partner banks. Alinea is an SEC-registered investment adviser whose brokerage accounts are held at DriveWealth, a FINRA and SIPC member, with protection up to $500,000 (including $250,000 for cash). SIPC protects you if a broker fails, not against investment losses.

Who Should Use Acorns?

  • Beginners who want to invest without making decisions
  • Anyone who wants an IRA (and a first-year match) in the same app
  • Android users, or anyone who wants web access
  • Parents who want investing accounts for their kids (Gold)

Who Should Use Alinea?

  • iPhone users who want to pick themes, stocks and crypto themselves
  • New investors who learn better with an AI assistant explaining their holdings
  • People with enough invested that $120 a year is a small share of their balance
  • Investors who already have retirement savings elsewhere and want a taxable "interests" account

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Acorns or Alinea cheaper?▼
Acorns Bronze is cheaper at $4 a month ($48 a year). Acorns Silver is $8 a month ($96 a year) and Gold is $12 a month ($144 a year). Alinea has one plan at $120 a year after a 7-day free trial. Neither charges trading commissions.
Does Alinea have a Roth IRA?▼
Alinea's App Store listing announces Roth and Traditional IRAs, but several 2026 reviews still describe Alinea as a taxable brokerage account only, so confirm availability in the app. Alinea does not offer an IRA match. Acorns offers Traditional, Roth and SEP IRAs on every plan, with a 1% (Silver) or 3% (Gold) first-year match on new contributions.
Is Alinea safe and legit?▼
Yes. Alinea is an SEC-registered investment adviser, and its brokerage accounts are held at DriveWealth, a FINRA and SIPC member, with SIPC protection up to $500,000 including $250,000 for cash. SIPC covers broker failure, not investment losses.
Can you buy crypto on Acorns or Alinea?▼
Alinea lets you buy cryptocurrency alongside stocks and ETFs. Acorns does not offer direct crypto; its portfolios can include bitcoin exposure only through a bitcoin-linked ETF.
Is Alinea available on Android?▼
No. Alinea is an iPhone app and has no Android app or web version. Acorns is available on iPhone, Android and the web.
Which is better for beginners, Acorns or Alinea?▼
Acorns is better for beginners who want automatic, hands-off investing plus retirement and checking in one app at a lower entry price. Alinea is better for beginners on iPhone who want to choose themes, stocks and crypto with an AI assistant, and who will invest enough that $120 a year is a small share of their balance.
