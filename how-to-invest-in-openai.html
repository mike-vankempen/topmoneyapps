How to Invest in OpenAI (2026): Can You Buy OpenAI Stock?

📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 6 min read

📋 Sources: reporting via CNBC, SmartAsset, and Forge as of November 2026

Short answer: you can't buy OpenAI stock directly — it's still a private company with no public shares. OpenAI reportedly filed a confidential draft IPO registration in 2026, but its CFO has said it will likely go public in 2027, not now, and no ticker, price, or date is confirmed. This guide covers exactly where things stand, the only legitimate ways to get exposure, and why so many "buy OpenAI shares" offers are scams.

  • For most people, the accessible route is indirect: Microsoft is OpenAI's largest strategic partner and investor, so owning Microsoft (MSFT) gives you a slice of exposure — though OpenAI is only a small part of Microsoft's overall value.

Can You Invest in OpenAI Right Now?

Not as an ordinary investor. OpenAI is private — there is no OpenAI ticker and no public shares. Reporting indicates OpenAI confidentially submitted a draft S-1 to the SEC in mid-2026, and its CFO has publicly said OpenAI will be a public company in 2027, "or sooner" if the business keeps accelerating. Until an actual IPO prices and lists, you cannot buy OpenAI on a normal brokerage.

Scam warning: "How to buy OpenAI stock" is one of the most-searched investing phrases, which makes it a magnet for fraud. There is no legitimate way for the general public to buy OpenAI shares today. Any app, DM, email, or ad offering "OpenAI pre-IPO shares" or an "OpenAI token" should be treated as a scam.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you invest in OpenAI in 2026?

Not directly — OpenAI is private with no public stock. It reportedly filed a confidential draft IPO registration, but there's no ticker, price, or date, and its CFO points to 2027. The only legitimate routes are accredited pre-IPO platforms or a public backer like Microsoft.

Is OpenAI publicly traded?

No. OpenAI does not trade on any exchange and has no ticker. Anyone selling "OpenAI stock" to the public is misrepresenting it — a common pre-IPO scam.

When will OpenAI go public?

No confirmed date. A confidential draft S-1 was reportedly filed in 2026, and the CFO has said OpenAI will be public in 2027, or sooner if growth accelerates. Treat specific dates online as speculation.

What is OpenAI's valuation?

Roughly $852 billion in its latest private round, confirmed by an August 2026 employee buyback at the same level. Talk of ~$1 trillion-plus is speculative.

How can I get OpenAI exposure before the IPO?

Indirectly, mainly through Microsoft (MSFT), OpenAI's largest partner — though OpenAI is a small part of Microsoft. Direct pre-IPO shares exist on accredited platforms (Forge, EquityZen) but are scarce and risky.
