Average Net Worth at 25 (2026)

The honest answer: at 25, most people have very little net worth — and often less than zero. The Federal Reserve's youngest bracket is "under 35," where the median is about $39,000 and the mean about $183,500 — but those are inflated by people in their early 30s with homes and years of saving. For a typical 25-year-old specifically, the real figure is far lower, and negative net worth is completely normal thanks to student loans. Here's the context and what actually matters at this age.

Why the "Average" Is Misleading at 25

The Federal Reserve reports net worth by age bracket, and the youngest is under 35 — a wide range that lumps a 25-year-old in with 34-year-olds who may own a home and have a decade of 401(k) contributions. That drags the bracket's median ($39,000) and mean ($183,500) well above the reality for someone exactly 25. Treat those as the ceiling of your cohort, not the midpoint for your age.

Negative Net Worth at 25 Is Normal

Net worth is everything you own minus everything you owe. Early in your career the "owe" side — student loans, a car loan, maybe credit-card debt — often outweighs the savings you've had time to build. A negative number at 25 isn't a failure; it's the starting line for most people. What matters is the direction of travel: debt shrinking, savings growing, retirement contributions beginning.

The one number that matters at 25: not your net worth, but your savings rate — the share of income you keep and invest. A positive, rising savings rate at 25 beats a big net worth you inherited or lucked into, because it compounds for the next four decades.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average net worth at age 25?

The Fed's youngest bracket is "under 35," with a median of about $39,000 and mean of $183,500 — but those skew high. For a typical 25-year-old, net worth is far lower, often near zero or negative.

Is negative net worth normal at 25?

Yes. Student loans and other debt commonly outweigh early savings. A negative number at 25 is a normal starting line — the trajectory matters, not the figure.

What is a good net worth at 25?

No single target. Getting positive, building a starter emergency fund, and beginning retirement contributions put you ahead. A common milestone is ~0.5–1× salary saved by 30, so at 25 you're still building toward it.

How do I build net worth in my 20s?

Pay down high-interest debt, build a small emergency fund, capture your 401(k) match, and start investing early. Time is your biggest advantage — money invested at 25 has 40+ years to grow.
