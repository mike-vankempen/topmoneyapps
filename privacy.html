Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

TopMoneyApps ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit topmoneyapps.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

TopMoneyApps is a content website. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you to submit personal information to read our articles. The information we do collect falls into two categories:

  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, approximate location (country/region), device and browser type, referral source, and on-page interactions. This data is aggregated and used to improve our content.
  • Information you choose to send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this only to respond to you.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies, primarily through Google Analytics, to measure traffic and usage. By default our analytics consent settings grant analytics storage but deny advertising storage, ad personalization, and ad user data — meaning we do not use your data for advertising personalization. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics across all sites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Affiliate Disclosure

TopMoneyApps participates in affiliate programs. Some links to third-party apps and services are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you sign up or make a purchase after clicking them — at no additional cost to you. Affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial scores or rankings, which are determined independently by our editorial team. When you click an affiliate link, the destination company may set its own cookies and collect information under its own privacy policy.

Third-Party Links and Services

Our articles link to third-party websites and apps (for example, brokerages and budgeting apps we review). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third party before providing them with personal information. We rely on the following third-party services that may process limited data: Google Analytics (analytics) and our hosting/content-delivery providers.

How We Use Information

  • To measure and improve site performance and content
  • To understand which topics and apps are most useful to readers
  • To respond to messages you send us
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse

Data Retention

Aggregated analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings. Emails you send us are retained only as long as needed to respond and maintain our records.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not collect directly identifying information through normal site use, these rights typically apply to any correspondence you've sent us. To make a request, email us at the address below. You can also limit data collection at any time through your browser's cookie controls or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

TopMoneyApps is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
