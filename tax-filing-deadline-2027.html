When Are Taxes Due in 2027? Key IRS Deadlines

By Mike Van Kempen Updated November 2026 6 min read

General information, not tax advice. The April 15, 2027 deadline is from the IRS; other dates are from tax-preparer calendars listed under Sources. Confirm your own dates with the IRS or a tax professional.

Federal income tax returns for 2026 are due Thursday, April 15, 2027, according to the IRS. Any tax you owe is due the same day, even if you get an extension. Filing Form 4868 by April 15 extends the deadline to file, not to pay, until Friday, October 15, 2027. Below is every key 2027 date for individual filers, from the January estimated payment to the October extension deadline.

Key Takeaways

  • The IRS says the tax deadline for 2026 tax returns is April 15, 2027, which falls on a Thursday.
  • Filing Form 4868 by April 15, 2027 extends the deadline to file a 2026 return to October 15, 2027, but tax owed is still due April 15.
  • The fourth-quarter 2026 estimated tax payment is due January 15, 2027; 2027 estimated payments are due April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2027, with the last one in January 2028.
  • Contributions to an IRA or HSA for tax year 2026 can be made until April 15, 2027, not including extensions.
  • As of November 2026, the IRS had not announced the date it will start accepting 2026 returns.

April 15, 2027: The Main Filing and Payment Deadline

The IRS states that the tax deadline for 2026 tax returns is April 15, 2027. Two things happen that day:

  • Your return is due. This is the Form 1040 covering income you earned from January 1 to December 31, 2026, taxed under the 2026 tax brackets and 2026 standard deduction.
  • Your payment is due. The IRS says that if you file an extension, you must still pay the taxes you owe by the deadline. Paying late adds interest and penalties.

If you expect a refund, filing early gets your money sooner. Our average tax refund by state page shows what filers typically receive.

Need More Time? The October 15, 2027 Extension

Filing Form 4868 by April 15, 2027 gives you an automatic six-month extension, which moves your filing deadline to Friday, October 15, 2027. The catch is that the extension covers paperwork only: estimate what you owe and pay it by April 15 to limit interest and penalties.

Extension to file is not an extension to pay. The IRS, Jackson Hewitt, Taxfyle and AE Tax Advisors all make the same point: tax owed for 2026 is due April 15, 2027 whether or not you file Form 4868.

IRA and HSA Contributions for 2026

You can still contribute to a traditional or Roth IRA for tax year 2026 until April 15, 2027. Extensions do not extend this deadline. The same April 15 cutoff applies to 2026 health savings account (HSA) contributions. When you contribute between January 1 and April 15, tell your provider which tax year the money is for. See the 2026 contribution limits for how much you can put in, and HSA vs. FSA for how the accounts differ.

Other Rules That Can Change Your Deadline

  • Weekends and holidays: when a deadline falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, it moves to the next business day. That is why the W-2 deadline moves to February 1, 2027.
  • Living abroad: U.S. citizens and resident aliens living outside the country get an automatic two-month extension to file, to June 15, 2027. Taxfyle notes you attach a statement explaining that you qualify.
  • Disasters and combat zones: the IRS says you may have more time if you were affected by a federally declared disaster or were serving in a designated combat zone. Postponements are announced area by area, so check the IRS disaster relief page for your county.
  • When filing opens: the IRS had not announced the 2027 filing season start date as of November 2026. Jackson Hewitt says the IRS typically starts accepting returns in late January.

Frequently Asked Questions

When are taxes due in 2027?

Federal income tax returns for tax year 2026 are due Thursday, April 15, 2027. Any tax you owe is also due that day.

What is the tax extension deadline in 2027?

If you file Form 4868 by April 15, 2027, you have until Friday, October 15, 2027 to file your 2026 return. The extension does not give you more time to pay.

When are estimated tax payments due in 2027?

The fourth-quarter 2026 payment is due January 15, 2027. Payments for 2027 are due April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2027, and the final 2027 payment is due in January 2028.

Can I still contribute to an IRA for 2026 in 2027?

Yes. You can make 2026 traditional or Roth IRA contributions until April 15, 2027. Extensions do not extend this deadline. The same cutoff applies to 2026 HSA contributions.

When can I start filing my 2026 taxes?

As of November 2026, the IRS had not announced the 2027 filing season start date. Jackson Hewitt says the IRS typically starts accepting returns in late January.
