How to File Your Taxes for Free: Every Option for 2027

By Mike Van Kempen Updated November 2026 7 min read

General information, not tax advice. Program rules are from the IRS; company offers are from each company's own site, checked November 2026. Confirm eligibility before you start.

Most people can file a federal tax return for free. If your adjusted gross income (AGI) was $89,000 or less for 2025, the IRS said you qualified for IRS Free File guided software, and Free File Fillable Forms has no income limit at all. Free in-person help is available through VITA (generally for people who make about $70,244 or less) and TCE (age 60 and older), and service members can use MilTax. Below is who qualifies for each option, what each one leaves out and what has changed for the 2027 filing season.

Key Takeaways

  • IRS Free File guided software was available to taxpayers with adjusted gross income of $89,000 or less in 2025; as of November 2026 the IRS had not announced the limit for 2026 returns.
  • Free File Fillable Forms lets anyone e-file a federal return at no cost regardless of income, but it does not prepare state returns and has limited error checking.
  • The IRS VITA program offers free tax preparation to people who generally make about $70,244 or less, people with disabilities and limited English speakers; TCE serves people age 60 and older.
  • IRS Direct File was not available in the 2026 filing season, and as of November 2026 the IRS had not announced a relaunch.
  • Both IRS Free File and Free File Fillable Forms were closed in November 2026, and the IRS says to check back in January 2027.

Option 1: IRS Free File Guided Software

IRS Free File is a public-private partnership between the IRS and the Free File Alliance, a group of tax software companies. You start on IRS.gov, pick a partner and fill out your return with the same kind of step-by-step interview you would get from paid software.

  • Income limit: for returns covering 2025, the IRS said guided Free File was open to taxpayers with an AGI of $89,000 or less. AGI is your total income minus certain adjustments, such as deductible IRA contributions.
  • Partner rules: each partner sets its own eligibility requirements, which may include age, income, state residency and military status. Read each offer before you choose one.
  • State returns: the IRS says free state return offers are available for most states.
  • Current year only: you can file only your current-year return through Free File, not a past year's.
  • Spanish: qualifying taxpayers can file in Spanish with partner EzTaxReturn.com.

Timing: as of November 2026, the IRS Free File page said "IRS Free File has closed" and to check back in January 2027. Last season it opened on January 9, 2026, ahead of the IRS filing season start on January 26. The IRS had not announced the income limit for 2026 returns.

Option 2: Free File Fillable Forms (No Income Limit)

Free File Fillable Forms is the IRS's electronic version of the paper forms. The IRS describes it as a way to "prepare and e-file your current-year federal tax return at no cost using online IRS forms," and it is available regardless of income. It suits you if your income is above the Free File limit and you are comfortable doing your own return.

The trade-offs, per the IRS:

  • It prepares a federal return only. It does not prepare a state return.
  • It performs basic calculations and has limited error checking.
  • It does not ask interview questions or explain which tax benefits apply to you.
  • Some forms, statements and attachments are not supported.
  • You need a 10-digit U.S. cell phone number that can receive text messages, and mobile devices are not recommended.

Last season, Fillable Forms opened on January 26, 2026. In November 2026 the IRS page said the program was closed until January 2027.

Option 3: Free Help From VITA and TCE Volunteers

If you would rather have someone prepare your return, the IRS funds two volunteer programs:

  • VITA (Volunteer Income Tax Assistance): free preparation for people who generally make about $70,244 or less, people with disabilities and limited English-speaking taxpayers. The IRS does not set a program-wide income limit, but individual sites may set their own.
  • TCE (Tax Counseling for the Elderly): free help for taxpayers 60 and older. Most TCE sites are run by AARP Tax-Aide.

To find a site, use the IRS VITA Locator Tool or call 800-906-9887. For AARP Tax-Aide, use the AARP site locator or call 888-227-7669; the IRS notes those sites operate January through April. Check what documents your site asks you to bring before you go.

Option 4: MilTax for Military Families

MilTax, offered through Military OneSource, lets active-duty and reserve members, including the Coast Guard, and certain veterans prepare and e-file a federal return and up to five state returns for free, according to the IRS. Because it covers up to five state returns, it is worth a look if you lived in more than one state during the year.

What Happened to IRS Direct File?

IRS Direct File was the IRS's own free online filing tool. In November 2025, the Associated Press reported an IRS email to state officials saying "IRS Direct File will not be available in Filing Season 2026. No launch date has been set for the future." The same report said 296,531 returns were accepted through Direct File in 2025. As of November 2026, we found no IRS announcement that it will return for the 2027 season, so plan on one of the options above.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I file my taxes for free?

Yes, most people can. IRS Free File guided software was free for taxpayers with an AGI of $89,000 or less for 2025 returns, Free File Fillable Forms is free regardless of income, and VITA, TCE and MilTax offer free help to people who qualify.

What is the income limit for IRS Free File?

For returns covering 2025, the IRS set the limit at an adjusted gross income of $89,000 or less. As of November 2026, the IRS had not announced the limit for 2026 returns filed in 2027. Each Free File partner can also set its own age, state and income rules.

Is IRS Direct File available in 2027?

IRS Direct File was not available in the 2026 filing season, and the IRS said no future launch date had been set. As of November 2026, we found no IRS announcement that it will return for the 2027 season.

Who qualifies for free VITA tax help?

The IRS says VITA serves people who generally make about $70,244 or less, people with disabilities and limited English-speaking taxpayers. Individual sites may set their own income limits. Find a site with the IRS VITA Locator Tool or by calling 800-906-9887.

Does Free File Fillable Forms include a state return?

No. Free File Fillable Forms prepares a federal return only. You need to file your state return separately, for example through your state tax agency or a Free File partner offer that includes your state.
