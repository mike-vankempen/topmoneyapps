How to Buy Anthropic Stock on Robinhood (ANT): IPO Access Steps, Price & Date

📅 Updated November 10, 2026 ⏱ 6 min read ✍️ By Mike Van Kempen

📋 Sources: Anthropic's amended S-1 (Nov. 10, 2026) as reported by Yahoo Finance and NerdWallet · Robinhood Support

You can't buy Anthropic stock on Robinhood yet, because it isn't trading. Anthropic plans to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, with a proposed IPO price of $70 to $78 per share and a first trading day reported for November 19, 2026. There are two ways to buy it on Robinhood: request shares at the IPO price through IPO Access before it lists, or buy ANT like any other stock once trading begins. This guide walks through both.

Can You Buy Anthropic Stock on Robinhood Right Now?

No. Anthropic is not a public stock yet. The company filed its S-1 registration statement on October 26, 2026, and on November 10 it amended the filing to set a price range of $70 to $78 per Class A share. Until the IPO prices and the stock begins trading on the Nasdaq, there is no ANT share to buy on Robinhood or any other brokerage.

What you can do before the listing is request shares at the IPO price. Robinhood runs a program called IPO Access that lets customers ask for shares in an offering before trading starts. Robinhood is among the platforms named in Anthropic's prospectus for retail distribution, according to NerdWallet's review of the filing. Whether the offering appears for your account, and how many shares you receive, is up to Robinhood and the underwriters.

Scam warning: Anthropic has warned about unauthorized sales of its stock. Any offer of "pre-IPO Anthropic shares" by email, social media or messaging app should be treated as a scam. The only legitimate routes are a brokerage's IPO program before the listing or buying ANT on the Nasdaq after it.

Option 1: Request Anthropic Shares at the IPO Price (IPO Access)

IPO Access is how Robinhood customers can receive shares at the offer price instead of buying after the first-day move. Robinhood says there is no added commission and no account minimum. These are the steps from Robinhood's own support pages:

  1. Open and fund a Robinhood account. You need an individual investing account. Robinhood says retirement, joint, custodial and managed accounts are not eligible for IPO Access. Open a Robinhood account here if you don't have one, and leave time for your identity check and bank transfer to clear.
  2. Find the IPO. In the app or on the web, go to Search and select the IPO filter, then choose Anthropic. If you have IPO notifications turned on, the announcement link also takes you there.
  3. Tap "I'm interested" and confirm eligibility. Robinhood shows a checklist. Certain financial-industry employees are restricted under FINRA rules.
  4. Read the prospectus. It is the official document describing the business, the share classes and the risks.
  5. Tap "Request shares" and enter how many you want. This creates a conditional offer to buy. It works like a pending buy order and does not guarantee you any shares. Robinhood says asking for more shares does not improve your odds of being picked.
  6. Review and submit. You can edit or cancel the request until the confirmation window closes after final pricing.
  7. Watch for the final price. If the final price lands within 20% of the range, Robinhood says you don't need to re-confirm. You can still cancel before the window closes.
  8. Check your allocation on listing day. Robinhood says shares are generally allocated after the market opens but before the stock starts trading. You may get everything you asked for, part of it, or nothing. Money held for unfilled shares returns to your buying power.

Know the flipping rule: Robinhood treats selling IPO Access shares within 30 days as "flipping," which can lock you out of IPO Access for 60 days. You are allowed to sell; it just affects future IPO requests.

Option 2: Buy ANT on Robinhood After It Starts Trading

Most people who want Anthropic stock will buy it this way. Once ANT is live on the Nasdaq, it trades on Robinhood like any other stock.

  1. Fund your account ahead of time so the cash is settled before listing day.
  2. Search "ANT" and confirm it is the Nasdaq listing for Anthropic. An unrelated company uses the ANT symbol on a Canadian exchange.
  3. Expect a late open. Large IPOs often do not print their first trade until late morning or midday Eastern time, after the opening auction.
  4. Use a limit order. A market order on a volatile first day can fill far above the price you saw. A limit order sets the most you will pay.
  5. Size it as speculative money. Keep any position small relative to your overall portfolio.

Risks to Weigh Before You Buy

We don't take a position on whether ANT is a good buy. These are the points each side raises:

Why some are interested

  • Third-quarter 2026 revenue of about $17 billion, per the filing
  • An unusually large retail allocation of up to 20%
  • $26 billion of concurrent investment from suppliers including Google and Nvidia
  • Few large pure-play AI companies are public

Why to be cautious

  • A valuation near or above $1 trillion leaves little room for error
  • Anthropic reported a net loss of about $104 billion for the first nine months of 2026, mostly non-cash charges, per NerdWallet
  • Class A holders have limited voting influence; the filing describes a "controlled company"
  • Recent mega-IPOs have traded below their offer price at times, and first-day swings can be sharp

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy Anthropic stock on Robinhood?

Not yet. Anthropic stock is not trading as of November 10, 2026. It plans to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, with trading reported to begin November 19, 2026. Before then, eligible Robinhood customers can request shares at the IPO price through IPO Access. After it lists, ANT can be bought on Robinhood like any other stock.

What is the Anthropic stock ticker?

Anthropic plans to list its Class A shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT. An unrelated company uses the ANT symbol on a Canadian exchange, so confirm you are looking at the Nasdaq listing.

When does Anthropic stock start trading?

CNBC reports that pricing is expected on November 18, 2026 and trading on November 19, citing people familiar with the plans. The Wall Street Journal reports a November 19 to 20 window. Anthropic has not confirmed a date.

How much will Anthropic stock cost?

Anthropic set a proposed IPO price range of $70 to $78 per share in an amended filing on November 10, 2026. The final price is set the night before trading begins and can fall outside that range. Once trading starts, the market price can be higher or lower than the IPO price.

How do I request Anthropic IPO shares on Robinhood?

In the Robinhood app, open Search, select the IPO filter and choose the company. Tap I'm interested, confirm eligibility, read the prospectus, then tap Request shares and submit. This creates a conditional offer to buy, which you can edit or cancel until the confirmation window closes after final pricing.

Am I guaranteed Anthropic shares through Robinhood IPO Access?

No. Robinhood says allocation is random among eligible requests, and that requesting more shares does not improve your odds. You may receive all, some or none of the shares you request.

Can I sell Anthropic IPO shares right away on Robinhood?

Yes, but Robinhood treats selling IPO Access shares within 30 days as flipping, which can restrict you from IPO Access for 60 days. Shares bought after the stock starts trading have no such restriction.

Can I buy Anthropic stock in a Robinhood IRA?

Not through IPO Access. Robinhood says retirement, joint, custodial and managed accounts are not eligible for IPO Access. Once ANT is trading, it can be bought in other Robinhood account types that support Nasdaq stocks.
