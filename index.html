Frequently asked questions

What is the best budgeting app in 2026?+

Empower ranks first in our budgeting scores (4.33 out of 5) because its dashboard is free and its app is rated 4.8 on the App Store. Monarch Money (3.95) is our top paid pick, with budgets, investment tracking and net worth in one app for $99.99 a year. YNAB (3.58) is best for zero-based budgeting; it costs $109 a year after a 34-day free trial.

What is the best investing app for beginners in 2026?+

Fidelity ranks first in our investing scores (4.93 out of 5): no commissions, no account minimums, fractional shares and 24/7 phone support. Beginners who want automated, hands-off investing can look at Betterment (4.13), and those who want spare change invested automatically can use Acorns (3.87), which costs $4 to $12 a month.

What is the best app for stock trading in 2026?+

Fidelity and Charles Schwab share the top investing score (4.93 out of 5); both offer $0 commissions, strong research and 24/7 phone support. Webull (4.27) suits active traders who want advanced charts and extended-hours trading. Robinhood (4.00) is free and simple, but its app has the lowest App Store rating of the major brokers (4.3).

What is the best app for ETF investing in 2026?+

Fidelity is the top ETF platform in our scores, with $0 commissions and zero-expense-ratio index funds such as FZROX. Vanguard (4.35) is the index fund pioneer and suits long-term buy-and-hold investors. M1 Finance (3.92) lets you build an auto-rebalancing "pie" of ETFs; it charges $3 a month unless you hold $10,000 or more.

What is the best app for crypto investing in 2026?+

Kraken ranks first in our crypto scores (4.07 out of 5), mainly for lower fees on Kraken Pro. Coinbase (4.02) is a close second, with 250+ coins and a beginner-friendly app but higher fees on simple trades. Robinhood is an easy way to get casual crypto exposure alongside stocks. Crypto held on an exchange is not FDIC- or SIPC-insured.

What is the best robo-advisor app in 2026?+

Betterment and Wealthfront tie in our investing scores (4.13 out of 5). Betterment offers automated tax-loss harvesting and goal-based investing for 0.25% a year (or $4 a month on smaller balances without recurring deposits); Wealthfront adds direct indexing and a $500 minimum. Acorns (3.87) is a flat-fee alternative for small, automatic investments.

What is the best app for options trading in 2026?+

tastytrade is built for options traders, with commissions capped at $10 per leg. Webull offers commission-free options with solid charting tools, and thinkorswim from Charles Schwab is the most powerful platform for advanced options analysis.

What is the best app for IRA and retirement investing in 2026?+

Fidelity is the top IRA platform in our scores, with no account minimums, zero-expense-ratio index funds and strong retirement planning tools. Robinhood matches IRA contributions (1%, or 3% with Robinhood Gold), and Acorns matches 1% to 3% of new contributions in your first year on its Silver and Gold plans. Betterment is the leading robo-advisor IRA at 0.25% a year.

What is the best app for real estate investing in 2026?+

Fundrise ranks first in our real estate scores (3.83 out of 5): a $10 minimum, diversified real estate funds and an IRA option, with about 1% a year in fees. Arrived (3.33) sells shares of individual rental homes from $100, and Groundfloor (3.33) offers short-term real estate loans from $10. None of these investments are FDIC- or SIPC-insured.

What is the best no-fee banking app in 2026?+

SoFi ranks first in our banking scores (4.72 out of 5): it is a chartered bank with no monthly fees, a 3.30% APY savings rate and built-in investing. Chime (4.33) has no monthly fees, early direct deposit and SpotMe fee-free overdraft coverage up to $200.

Is Chase Sapphire Reserve or Amex Platinum better in 2026?+

Chase Sapphire Reserve ($795/year) is better for dining and everyday travel rewards — 3x points on dining and travel, flexible points transferable to 14 airline and hotel partners. Amex Platinum ($895/year) wins for airport lounge access (1,400+ lounges worldwide) and frequent flyers who book directly with airlines (5x points on flights). CSR is the better all-around value; Amex Platinum is best for ultra-frequent flyers. In our credit card scores, Chase Sapphire Reserve (4.33) edges Amex Platinum (4.20), mainly on its lower annual fee; the no-fee Chase Freedom Flex (4.63) scores highest overall.

Which finance apps are completely free to use?+

Fidelity, Charles Schwab, Robinhood, Webull, Public.com, Chime, SoFi and Empower's dashboard are free to use with no monthly fees. M1 Finance charges $3 a month under $10,000. Acorns costs $4 to $12 a month, and Betterment charges 0.25% a year. YNAB costs $109 a year after a 34-day free trial, and Alinea $120 a year after a 7-day trial. Chase Sapphire Reserve has a $795 annual fee and Amex Platinum $895.

What should I look for in a personal finance app?+

When choosing a personal finance app, focus on five things: (1) Your primary goal — budgeting, investing, banking, or credit rewards each have specialist apps; (2) Cost — many great apps are free, but premium features may require a subscription; (3) Platform support — iOS, Android, or web; (4) Security — look for biometric login, 256-bit encryption, and FDIC or SIPC insurance; (5) Reputation — check App Store, Google Play, and Trustpilot reviews from verified users.
