The nuance: the total is growing (record $1.01T), but much of that is higher prices, not more gifts. Per-person gift budgets are actually roughly flat to slightly down. So "spending is up" and "my budget is tighter" can both be true this year.

Is Holiday Spending Rising?

Total spending has set records for several years running, driven by a mix of real demand and higher prices. Adobe expects online spending alone to hit $275.1 billion (up 6.7%), with Cyber Monday the single biggest online day at about $15.1 billion. The catch worth watching: buy-now-pay-later is forecast at $21.3 billion this season — convenient, but a fast way to carry holiday debt into the new year if you're not careful.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do Americans spend on the holidays in 2026?

The NRF projects about $1.01 trillion in total U.S. holiday retail sales (Nov–Dec), up 4.2%. Per person, PwC's 2026 outlook finds about $708 on gifts, while the NRF's all-in seasonal figure (gifts, food, décor) is about $890. The number you see depends on what the survey counts.

What is the average gift budget per person in 2026?

About $708 on gifts per person per PwC — down roughly 2% year over year. Surveys that include food, travel, and decorations land higher (around $890). For gifts alone, $700–$1,000 is a reasonable range depending on income and household size.

Is holiday spending going up or down?

The total is up (record $1.01T, +4.2%; online +6.7%), but a lot of that growth is higher prices rather than more purchases — and some per-person gift surveys show a slight dip. The pie is bigger while individual budgets are roughly flat.

How much is spent online during the holidays?

Adobe projects $275.1 billion online (Nov 1–Dec 31), up 6.7%. Cyber Monday is the biggest online day (~$15.1 billion), over half of purchases happen on mobile, and BNPL is forecast at $21.3 billion.

How can I spend less on the holidays?

Set a total gift budget and split it per person, shop the Cyber Week sales, use a cash-back card and pay it in full, try a family gift exchange, and avoid buy-now-pay-later unless you can clear it in the zero-interest window.
