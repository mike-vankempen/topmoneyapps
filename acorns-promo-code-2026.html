Is there an Acorns promo code?

Not in the traditional sense. Unlike retailers with a "promo code" field at checkout, Acorns pays its bonus through referral/invite links, not typed codes. When you create your account through a qualifying link, the $20 offer is attached to your sign-up automatically — there's nothing to copy and paste. So if you're hunting for a magic code to enter, you can stop: signing up through the link below and completing the requirement is the "code."

How to claim the Acorns $20 bonus

  1. Open a new account through a qualifying link. The $20 offer is for new Acorns users. Use the Acorns sign-up link — the bonus attaches automatically.
  2. Set up a Recurring Investment of at least $5. This is the key requirement. Deposit $5+ into an Acorns Invest, Early, Later, or Checking account using the Recurring Investment feature.
  3. Let the first investment process. Once your first successful recurring investment goes through, you've met the requirement.
  4. Get your $20. Acorns typically deposits the bonus within about 10 days of the following month. Do it in October and it usually lands in early-to-mid November.

Is the $20 bonus actually worth it?

Here's the honest math. Acorns charges a flat subscription starting at $4 a month, so the $20 bonus covers roughly five months of the base plan — a real head start. But that flat fee is a high percentage on a small balance, so the bonus is genuinely worth it in one situation: you actually plan to use Acorns to invest regularly.

  • Worth it if: you want hands-off, automatic investing with round-ups and you'll keep contributing. The $20 plus round-ups gets your balance moving from day one.
  • Less worth it if: you'd open an account just to grab $20 and cancel — after a month or two of the $4 fee, the net gain shrinks.
