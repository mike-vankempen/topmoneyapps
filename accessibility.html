Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 17, 2026

TopMoneyApps, operated by Charlie Labs, Inc., is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Our Commitment & Conformance Target

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive impairments. WCAG 2.1 AA is the standard commonly referenced under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

Measures We Take

To support accessibility, our site includes:

  • Semantic HTML structure with landmarks (navigation, main content, footer) and a "skip to main content" link;
  • Keyboard operability for interactive elements, with a visible focus indicator;
  • Text and background color combinations that meet WCAG AA contrast ratios;
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images, and appropriate labels for controls;
  • Respect for the "reduced motion" preference to minimize animation for those who need it;
  • Responsive layouts that work across screen sizes and support browser zoom and text resizing.

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, not a one-time project. We regularly review new and existing content, and we work to remediate issues as they are identified. Some third-party content or embedded tools may not always be fully under our control; where that is the case, we seek accessible alternatives or provide assistance on request.

Need Help or Found a Barrier?

If you have difficulty accessing any part of TopMoneyApps, or you need information in an alternative format, please contact us and we will work promptly to help and to fix the issue. Email editorial@topmoneyapps.com with the page address and a description of the problem. We aim to respond within a few business days.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of TopMoneyApps. Your input helps us improve. Please tell us if you encounter any barriers, and let us know how we could make the site work better for you.
