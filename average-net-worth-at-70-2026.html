Average Net Worth at 70 (2026)

📅 Updated November 2026 ⏱ 6 min read 📊 Source: Federal Reserve (SCF)

📋 Source: Federal Reserve — Survey of Consumer Finances (households aged 65–74, latest release)

At 70, the average household net worth is about $1.79 million — the highest of any age group, and the reason it's misleading. The median (the true middle) is roughly $410,000, because a wealthy minority pulls the average far above where most retirees actually stand. Mean vs. Median: Why the Gap Matters

The $1.79M average and the $410K median describe the same group — so why the four-fold gap? Because net worth is extremely top-heavy. A relatively small number of very wealthy households pull the average way up, while the median (the household standing exactly in the middle) reflects a far more ordinary reality. If you're comparing yourself to anything, compare to the median — it's the honest benchmark for a typical 70-year-old.

Why Net Worth Peaks at 65–74

This age group has the highest average net worth of any in the survey, and it's no accident. By 70, most households have had a full working life to save, mortgages are often paid off or nearly so, and retirement accounts are at or near their peak. After 75, net worth typically starts to decline as people draw down savings and healthcare costs rise. In other words, 70 is roughly the financial high-water mark for most Americans.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average net worth at age 70?

For ages 65–74, the average (mean) is about $1.79 million — the highest of any age group — but the median is about $410,000, and the median is the realistic benchmark since a wealthy minority skews the average up.

What is a good net worth at 70?

Beating the ~$410,000 median puts you ahead of half your peers; ~$1.2 million is around the top 25%. What matters most is whether your savings plus Social Security cover your spending for life.

Why does net worth peak at 65–74?

A lifetime of saving, paid-off mortgages, and peak retirement balances — before later-life spending and healthcare costs start reducing wealth after 75.

Does net worth include my home and retirement accounts?

Yes — everything you own minus everything you owe: 401(k)s, IRAs, brokerage, cash, and home equity, minus debts. Home equity and retirement accounts are usually the two biggest pieces at 70.

How much do I need to retire comfortably at 70?

A common rule is the 4% rule: multiply your desired annual withdrawal by 25. $50,000/year from savings implies ~$1.25M, on top of Social Security. Your real number depends on spending and how long the money must last.
