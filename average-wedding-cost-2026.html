Average Wedding Cost in 2026: $34,200 and What It Covers

By Mike Van Kempen Updated November 2026 7 min read

Data from The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study (10,474 couples married in 2025) and Zola's 2026 reports, with marriage data from the CDC and Census Bureau. All checked November 2026.

The average wedding cost $34,200 in 2025, according to The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study, up from $33,000 in 2024. The average wedding had 117 guests, and the average cost per guest was $292. Zola, which surveys couples separately, puts the average 2026 wedding at $36,000 paid out of pocket by the couple. Below are the costs by category, the most and least expensive states and cities, who pays and how to set your own budget.

Key Takeaways

  • The average wedding in 2025 cost $34,200, according to The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study of 10,474 U.S. couples, up from $33,000 in 2024.
  • The average 2025 wedding had 117 guests, and the average cost per guest was $292, up $8 from 2024, according to The Knot.
  • Zola's 2026 First Look Report puts the average 2026 wedding at $36,000 with 145 guests, measured as what couples pay out of pocket after family contributions.
  • The Knot's most expensive state for weddings was New Jersey at $57,000, and the least expensive was Wyoming at $17,000.
  • The CDC counted 2,128,446 marriages in the U.S. in 2024 (provisional), a rate of 6.3 per 1,000 people.

Why The Knot and Zola Report Different Numbers

The two most-cited wedding surveys measure different things, so don't compare them directly:

  • The Knot ($34,200, 117 guests): the average cost of weddings held in 2025, from 10,474 couples recruited from The Knot and WeddingWire members.
  • Zola ($36,000, 145 guests): Zola says this is "the total amount couples pay out of pocket after any contributions from family," for weddings in 2026. Zola's First Look Report is based on a survey of more than 11,500 couples.

Both surveys come from wedding-planning platforms, so their respondents are couples who used those sites. Treat the figures as typical for that group rather than for every U.S. wedding.

Who Pays for the Wedding?

Family help is common, but most couples now put in their own money. Zola reports that 88% of couples contribute their own money: 29% pay for the wedding fully and 59% partially. Zola also found that for weddings over $100,000, families cover 63% of the total cost.

The Knot's data shows that weddings cost more when others chip in. Its 2025 averages by how much the couple paid:

How Many People Get Married Each Year?

Government data puts the survey numbers in context. The CDC's National Center for Health Statistics counted 2,128,446 marriages in the U.S. in 2024 (provisional), a rate of 6.3 per 1,000 people, up from 2,041,926 marriages (6.1 per 1,000) in 2023. People are also marrying later: the Census Bureau estimated the median age at first marriage for men at 30.8 in 2025, up from 23.5 in 1975.

How to Set a Wedding Budget

  • Start with the guest list. At The Knot's $292 average per guest, cutting 20 guests saves about $5,840 (our calculation).
  • Decide who is contributing before you book. Agree on amounts with family early so you know your real total.
  • Lock in the venue and catering first. They are the two biggest costs in both surveys, and they set the scale for everything else.
  • Save in a separate account. Keeping wedding money in a high-yield savings account earns interest and keeps it apart from your emergency fund.
  • Track spending together. A shared budgeting app helps you see every deposit and vendor payment. Compare options in our best budgeting apps for couples guide.
  • Avoid financing the wedding on credit cards. Card interest can turn a one-day event into years of payments; see our average credit card interest rate data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost of a wedding in 2026?

The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study found the average wedding cost $34,200 in 2025, the latest full year of data. Zola projects an average of $36,000 for 2026 weddings, measured as what couples pay out of pocket after family contributions.

How much does a wedding cost per guest?

The Knot found the average cost per guest was $292 in 2025, up $8 from 2024. The average wedding had 117 guests.

What is the most expensive part of a wedding?

The reception venue is the largest single cost, averaging $12,900 in The Knot's 2025 data. Catering averaged $80 per person, which adds up to several thousand dollars for a typical guest count.

Which state has the most expensive weddings?

In The Knot's 2025 data, New Jersey had the highest average wedding cost at $57,000, and Wyoming had the lowest at $17,000. New York City was the most expensive metro area at $88,000.

Who pays for the wedding?

Zola reports that 88% of couples contribute their own money, with 29% paying for the wedding fully and 59% partially. Families often help, especially with larger weddings.
