Webull vs. Public.com (2026): Which Trading App Is Better?

📅 Updated November 27, 2026 ⏱ 8 min read ✍️ By Mike Van Kempen

Webull is the better choice for active traders who want advanced tools, and Public.com is the better choice for options traders who want rebates and investors who want a published cash yield. Both charge $0 commission on U.S. stocks, ETFs and stock options and have no account minimum. Here is how they compare on fees, subscriptions, cash, IRAs and features.

Key Takeaways

  • Webull and Public.com both charge $0 commission on U.S. stocks, ETFs and stock options, with no account minimum.
  • Public pays a rebate of $0.06 to $0.18 per stock or ETF options contract, set monthly by trading volume.
  • Public's high-yield cash account paid 3.30% APY without a subscription, or 3.75% with its $20-a-month Reserve plan, as of September 17, 2026.
  • Webull Premium costs $40 a year and raises its IRA match to 3.5%; Public Reserve costs $200 a year and raises its IRA match to 4%.
  • Webull has a TopMoneyApps score of 4.27/5 and Public.com 4.12/5; both are rated 4.7 on the Apple App Store.

Webull ranks higher in our investing rankings, with a TopMoneyApps score 4.27/5 versus TopMoneyApps score 4.12/5 for Public.com. Both charge $0 commission on U.S. stocks, ETFs and stock options, both trade 24 hours a day five days a week, and both clear trades through Apex.

It also offers Treasuries, bonds and AI-built custom indexes.

Trading Fees

Stocks, ETFs and stock options are commission-free at both brokers. You still pay small regulatory fees on sales, such as the SEC fee, at either one. Public lists its pass-through options fees as $0.02 per contract for the options regulatory fee and $0.02 for the clearing fee.

The difference is the options rebate. Public pays $0.06 to $0.18 per stock or ETF options contract, set monthly by your trading volume, and says the rebate shares its payment for order flow revenue with members. Trades in SPY, QQQ and IWM earn $0.06 or $0.10. If you trade 200 contracts a month at the lowest rate, that's $12 back.

Index options cost $0.50 per contract on certain contracts at Webull, with Premium members paying tiered rates of $0.50, $0.40 or $0.30. Public charges $0.35 per index option contract.

Other fees to know:

  • Transfers out: $75 at Webull and $100 at Public. Incoming transfers are free at both.
  • Inactivity: Public charges $3.99 a month on accounts under $70 with no activity for six months.
  • Margin: Public's rate was 5.15% on balances up to $50,000, as of September 17, 2026. Webull's margin page did not show a clear current rate table when checked, so confirm in the app.

Subscriptions: Webull Premium vs. Public Reserve

Webull Premium costs $40 a year. It raises Webull's IRA contribution match from 1% to 3.5%, adds a 1% match on transfers and rollovers (capped at $2,500), cuts index option and futures fees, and includes real-time Level 2 market data. The match requires keeping Premium for a year and holding matched money for five years.

Public Reserve costs $20 a month or $200 a year. It raises Public's cash APY from 3.30% to 3.75% and its IRA contribution match from 1% to 4%, paid over 12 months with a five-year holding requirement. At $200 a year, the extra 0.45% on cash only pays for itself on balances above about $44,000, before counting the IRA match.

Cash and Retirement Accounts

Public's high-yield cash account pays 3.30% APY with no minimum and no subscription, and offers up to $5 million of FDIC coverage through partner banks (as of September 17, 2026). APY is the yearly return including compounding; see APR vs. APY. Webull's cash management page showed no rate when checked on November 27, 2026, so check the app for its current yield.

Both offer traditional and Roth IRAs with a 1% match on contributions. Public pays its standard match over 12 months and its 1% transfer match over 60 months. An IRA match is extra money from the broker that you can lose if you move the account too soon, so read the holding rules. For how IRAs work, see Roth IRA vs. traditional IRA.

Features and Investments

Webull

  • Trading tools: desktop and web platforms, two mobile apps and APIs.
  • Paper trading: practice with $100,000 of virtual money (per NerdWallet).
  • Custodial accounts: UTMA accounts for kids, without margin or options.
  • Futures and prediction markets (per NerdWallet), plus robo-advice through Webull Smart Advisor.
  • Crypto: no commission, but a 1% spread on each buy and sell, with a $2 minimum order.

Public.com

  • Bonds and Treasuries: Treasuries cost $0.10 to $0.25 per $100 of face value; corporate bonds $0.35 to $0.50.
  • Managed accounts with a $1,000 minimum: a Treasury Account, Direct Indexing (0.19% a year) and Generated Assets (0.49%), which builds a custom index from a text prompt and backtests it against the S&P 500.
  • Crypto through zerohash: flat fees from $0.49 on orders up to $10 to $6.29 on orders of $250 to $500, then 1.25% above $500. Public announced crypto trading in IRAs in March 2026.
  • No custodial accounts, according to NerdWallet.

Order Routing and Safety

Both brokers earn payment for order flow (PFOF), meaning market makers pay the broker to execute your trades. Public receives PFOF on its default stock route but not on its optional "Smart Order" route, which costs $0.003 per share. Webull also accepts PFOF, according to NerdWallet.

Both are SIPC members and clear through Apex, so securities are protected up to $500,000, including $250,000 in cash, if the broker fails. Webull also lists excess-SIPC coverage from Lloyd's of London underwriters. SIPC does not cover market losses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Webull or Public cheaper?▼
For stocks, ETFs and stock options both charge $0 commission. Public can be cheaper for options traders because it pays a rebate of $0.06 to $0.18 per stock or ETF options contract. Webull is cheaper to leave: its outgoing transfer fee is $75 versus $100 at Public.
Does Public really pay you to trade options?▼
Yes. Public pays a rebate of $0.06 to $0.18 per stock or ETF options contract, set monthly by your trading volume. Trades in SPY, QQQ and IWM earn $0.06 or $0.10. Public says the rebate shares its payment for order flow revenue with customers.
Which has a higher IRA match, Webull or Public?▼
Both pay 1% on IRA contributions for free accounts. With paid plans, Public Reserve ($200 a year) pays 4% and Webull Premium ($40 a year) pays 3.5%. Both require you to keep matched money in the account for five years.
Does Webull have paper trading?▼
Yes. Webull offers paper trading, which lets you practice with virtual money (NerdWallet puts it at $100,000) before you trade with real money.
Are Webull and Public safe?▼
Both are SIPC members that clear through Apex, so securities are protected up to $500,000, including $250,000 in cash, if the broker fails. Webull also lists excess-SIPC coverage. SIPC does not protect against investment losses.
